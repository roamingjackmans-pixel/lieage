How to play
  1. One screen hosts. Open Lieage on a TV, laptop or tablet everyone can see and tap Host a game. It shows a 5-number code. The host screen is the game itself, not a player.
  2. Everyone else joins on their phone with the code (2 to 10 players). The first player to join gets a 👑 and can start the game from their phone too.
  3. Each question is a strange but true fact with a word missing. Players take turns picking the topic.
  4. Everyone secretly writes a lie to fill the blank, something believable enough to fool the others. Stuck? Tap 💡 for an idea.
  5. All the lies get mixed in with the real answer. Find the truth on your phone. You can't pick your own lie.
  6. Scoring: find the truth for 500, and get 250 for every player who falls for your lie. Round 2 is worth double, and the final question triple.
  7. Tap 😂 on answers that make you laugh. At the end there are awards for the best liar, the best truth detector, the crowd favourite and the most gullible.
